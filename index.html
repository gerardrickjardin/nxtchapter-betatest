ABOUT NXT CHAPTER

Empowering Second Chances. Rebuilding Futures.

At NXT Chapter, we are dedicated to supporting individuals through every phase of reentry after incarceration. We provide immediate release stabilization — including assistance with vital state IDs, clothing, hygiene kits, and RTD bus passes — alongside holistic long-term peer mentorship, virtual reality job training, and direct workforce placement.

Our mission is rooted in second chances, lived experience, and community restoration. Every person deserves the tools, dignity, and opportunities to write their next chapter.

OUR CORE PILLARS

Built on Hope, Driven by Impact

Reentry Stabilization

Providing vital ID vouchers, emergency housing assistance, clothing, hygiene essentials, and immediate release support so individuals land on solid ground.

Peer Mentorship

Led by individuals with lived experience who understand the journey. Providing 1-on-1 guidance, emotional support, and youth development workshops.

SkillVRse Workforce Placement

Cutting-edge Virtual Reality job training, resume building, interview practice, and direct placement with top inclusive employer partners.

OUR MISSION & VISION

NXT Chapter exists to remove systemic barriers and build clear pathways for individuals taking their next step toward independence, dignity, and career fulfillment.

Our Core Pillars

  • Empowerment Through Education: Providing practical digital literacy, financial guidance, and career training.
  • Relational Mentorship: Connecting individuals with caring, experienced mentors who provide guidance and accountability.
  • Systemic Advocacy: Championing policies and partnerships that create equal opportunities for reentry and youth growth.
FOUNDED IN COMMUNITY

THE NXT CHAPTER STORY

Founded in Denver, NXT Chapter started with a simple belief: everyone deserves a second chance and a dedicated support system to help them write their next chapter in life.

From local park meetups and informal career workshops, we have grown into a multi-department organization serving over 500+ lives each year with a network of 20+ community partners.
